Blender

During Rochester Institute of Technology's SCOOP (Software Engineering Co-Op) program in Fall 2026, I contributed to Blender, an extremely popular, open-source 3D modeling software used by hobbyists and even movie studios. Blender has even been used in the production of movies such as Spider-Man: Across the Spiderverse and Everything Everywhere All At Once.

I have acted as the Project Manager (PM) in a team of 3 software engineers in an asynchronous remote setting. As the PM, my duties include directing the team, hosting daily standup meetings, managing documentation, and delegating tasks.

My team's main duty is to fix issues reported by Blender users. I have used VS Code's Copilot AI to find and analyze relevant code in Blender's large codebase, and I used VS Code's debugger (with breakpoints, conditional breakpoints, and logpoints) to step through functions and find relevant variables. Blender uses Git version control and a Gitea-based remote repository.
